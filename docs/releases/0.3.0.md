Local backup cleanup. Requires **openHAB 5.0 or later**.

## New
- **Backups to Keep on Server** (`localRetentionCount`): only the newest _N_ backups stay in the local backup folder, older ones are deleted after each upload and when the binding starts. 0 (default) keeps all, as before.
- Safety rule: a local backup is only deleted if it is in Google Drive or older than the newest backup in Google Drive. A backup that could not be uploaded yet (for example while the internet is down) is never deleted.

## Update from 0.2.0
1. Delete `org.openhab.binding.googledrive-0.2.0.jar` from the `addons` folder.
1. Copy `org.openhab.binding.googledrive-0.3.0.jar` (below) into the `addons` folder.
1. Things, items and the Google login are kept. The new setting appears in the **Backup Upload** thing.
